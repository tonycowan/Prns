# What's new

Since the last release.

Info sits beside a contact's name. It holds the name you use for them, and how often your location is shared: never, once a day, once an hour, or every 10 minutes. The back arrow returns to that journal. Remove is on that page. The contact list no longer slides.

Map sits between Add a contact and Your QR code. It shows the latest place for every contact who has shared one, and the time that place was current. Choosing a name zooms to that contact. A contact who has never shared a place is grey and is not on the map.

A location you chose to share on a schedule is sent while N10A is open. It updates the other person's map and does not appear as a note.
